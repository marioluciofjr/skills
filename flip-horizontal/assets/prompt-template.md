Substitua cada campo entre colchetes. Apague os colchetes. Não acrescente seções.

```text

Keep [his/her/their] face, identity, hair, expression, skin tone, clothing, colors, fabric, and body proportions exactly as in the reference photo; only the pose and the backdrop are new.

What: A new studio photo of this [personagem] standing in the [left/right] part of the frame, posed as follows. [Braço do gesto, em lado anatômico: cotovelo, trajeto do antebraço, onde a mão termina.] [Gesto: dedo estendido, outros dedos dobrados, apontando para the empty green space that fills the [lado] part of the frame, ângulo.] [Outro braço, em lado anatômico.] [Cabeça e olhar: turns toward the [lado] part of the frame; eyes glance into the same empty green space.] Exactly two arms and two hands, five fingers on each hand, each arm continuous from shoulder to elbow to wrist, [mangas, punhos e detalhes da roupa] matching the reference photo. Where: A flat, seamless chroma key [green (#00B140) / blue (#0047BB)] backdrop filling the entire frame behind [him/her/them], evenly lit, with no gradient, no shadow, no texture, no floor line, no props, and no text. The large empty [green/blue] area fills the [lado] part of the frame. No [green/blue] reflections or spill on [skin, hair, clothing]; clean, crisp edges around [cabelo, barba, contorno] for easy keying.

How: Photorealistic studio portrait, [ângulo de câmera], [enquadramento: ex. waist-up framing with the bottom edge cutting at the waist as in the reference photo], [luz: ex. soft diffused frontal lighting], [cores e tons de pele]. [Proporção e orientação: ex. 16:9 landscape image]. [Composição calculada: quanto do quadro o personagem ocupa, de que lado, espaço acima da cabeça, espaço vazio do lado do gesto.] If a second plain [green/blue] image is attached, use its exact aspect ratio as the canvas. Sharp focus, no watermark, no text.
```
